SECURITIES PROSPECTUS

I. ISSUING ENTITY

This Securities Prospectus is being issued by GlobalTech Industries, Inc., a leading technology company incorporated under the laws of the State of Delaware, with its headquarters located at 1234 Silicon Drive, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA. GlobalTech Industries, Inc. (the "Company") is a recognized leader in the development, manufacturing, and marketing of advanced technology solutions for a variety of industries.

II. TERMS OF THE OFFERING

The Company is offering for sale perpetual bonds (the "Bonds") with an indefinite maturity. The Bonds will be issued in denominations of $1,000 and integral multiples thereof. The Bonds will not have a specific maturity date but can be called by the Company at its option at any time after a specified call date.

III. COUPON RATE AND INTEREST PAYMENTS

The Bonds will bear a fixed coupon rate of 5.5% per annum, payable semi-annually on June 30 and December 31 of each year, commencing on December 31, 2023.

IV. CALL PRICE

The Bonds may be redeemed at the option of the Company at any time on or after December 31, 2030 (the "Call Date") at a call price equal to 100% of the principal amount plus accrued and unpaid interest.

V. CONVERSION FEATURES

The Bonds will not be convertible into equity shares of the Company.

VI. RISK FACTORS

Investing in the Bonds involves certain risks, including the following:

a. Interest rate risk - fluctuations in interest rates may adversely affect the market value of the Bonds;
b. Credit risk - the Company's ability to make interest payments and repay the principal amount is subject to its financial condition and earnings;
c. Liquidity risk - there may be limited liquidity in the market for the Bonds, making it difficult to sell them at fair value;
d. Redemption risk - the Company may redeem the Bonds at any time after the Call Date
