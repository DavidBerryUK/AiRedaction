CORPORATE GOVERNANCE GUIDELINES
DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION POLICY

1. INTRODUCTION

At XYZ Ltd, we are committed to promoting a diverse and inclusive workplace that values and respects the unique contributions of each employee. We believe that a diverse workforce drives innovation, enhances decision-making, and strengthens our ability to serve our customers and stakeholders. This policy sets out our commitment to promoting diversity and inclusion at all levels of the organization.

2. DEFINITIONS

For the purposes of this policy, the following definitions apply:

* Diversity: Refers to the variety of differences between people, including but not limited to race, gender, age, sexual orientation, religion, disability, and socio-economic background.
* Inclusion: Refers to the practice of creating a work environment where everyone feels valued, respected, and able to fully participate in the organization's activities.

3. COMMITMENT TO DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION

At XYZ Ltd, we are committed to:

* Fostering a culture of respect, tolerance, and inclusion.
* Providing equal opportunities for all employees, regardless of their background or personal characteristics.
* Encouraging diversity in all aspects of our business, including recruitment, hiring, promotion, and development.
* Providing a safe and welcoming workplace, free from discrimination, harassment, and bullying.
* Promoting diversity and inclusion in our supply chain, partnerships, and collaborations.

4. DIVERSITY TARGETS

At XYZ Ltd, we aim to:

* Achieve a gender balance of at least 40% women and 40% men in senior leadership positions by 2025.
* Increase the representation of ethnic minorities and other underrepresented groups in our workforce.
* Provide opportunities for employees with disabilities and those from socio-economically disadvantaged backgrounds.
