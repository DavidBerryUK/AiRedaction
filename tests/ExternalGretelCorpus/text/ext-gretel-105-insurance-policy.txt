DISABILITY INSURANCE POLICY

This Disability Insurance Policy (the "Policy") is entered into between Melanie Michael Mayer, policyholder, and SureGuard Insurance Company, policy issuer. The policy effective date is January 1, 2023. The policy terminates on December 31, 2028.

I. INSURANCE COVERAGE

The policy provides income protection in the event of disability, including both short-term and long-term coverage options.

A. Short-Term Disability Coverage

Short-term disability coverage provides income replacement for a period of up to 26 weeks due to a covered disability. The waiting period for short-term disability benefits is 7 calendar days.

B. Long-Term Disability Coverage

Long-term disability coverage provides income replacement for a period of up to 24 months due to a covered disability. The waiting period for long-term disability benefits is 180 calendar days.

III. PREMIUMS

The premium for this policy is $1,500 per month, payable on the first day of each month.

IV. POLICY ENDORSEMENTS

The following endorsements apply to this policy:

A. Waiver of Premium: If the policyholder becomes disabled, premiums will be waived after a waiting period of 90 days.

B. Partial Disability: The policy will pay benefits if the policyholder is unable to perform one or more essential job duties due to a covered disability.

C. Residual Disability: The policy will pay benefits if the policyholder experiences a loss of income due to a covered disability.

V. COVERAGE LIMITS

The maximum monthly benefit for short-term disability coverage is $5,000. The maximum monthly benefit for long-term disability coverage is $10,000.
