SECURITIES PROSPECTUS

I. ISSUING ENTITY

This Securities Prospectus is issued by the United Kingdom Housing Finance Agency (UKHFA), a government-sponsored entity established in 2002 to provide long-term funding for housing finance institutions in the United Kingdom.

II. TERMS OF THE OFFERING

We are offering £500,000,000 of Agency Bonds with a maturity of 10 years, due on 15th June 2032. The bonds will bear interest at a rate of 2.5% per annum, payable semi-annually on 15th June and 15th December of each year, commencing on 15th December 2022. The bonds will be issued at a price of 99.500%, and the minimum denomination for investment will be £10,000.

III. RISK FACTORS

Investing in the bonds involves certain risks, including but not limited to:

1. Credit Risk: Although UKHFA is a government-sponsored entity, there is a risk that the issuer may not be able to meet its debt obligations. However, the risk is considered low due to the issuer's backing by the UK government.

2. Interest Rate Risk: The value of the bonds may fluctuate due to changes in market interest rates. An increase in interest rates will cause the value of the bonds to decrease, and vice versa.

3. Liquidity Risk: The bonds may not be readily marketable, and there may be difficulty in selling the bonds at or near the original price.

IV. USE OF PROCEEDS

The proceeds from the bond issuance will be used to provide long-term funding for housing finance institutions in the UK. The funds will be used to support the development of affordable housing for low-income families and first-time homebuyers.

V. LEGAL AND REGULATORY INFORMATION

The bonds will be governed by the laws of England and Wales. The bonds will be offered and sold in accordance with the provisions of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 and the Prospectus Regulation Rules made
