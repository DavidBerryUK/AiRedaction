LOAN AGREEMENT

THIS LOAN AGREEMENT (the "Agreement"), dated as of the 1st day of January, 2023, is made and entered into by and between Lisa Samele-Bosurgi, a resident of 202 Laura Wells ("Borrower"), and The Educational Lending Institute, a Delaware corporation ("Lender").

WHEREAS, Borrower has applied for a student loan from Lender in the amount of $50,000 (the "Loan") to finance Borrower's education at The University of Toronto;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. LOAN AMOUNT. Lender agrees to loan to Borrower the principal amount of $50,000, which amount shall be disbursed directly to The University of Toronto.

2. INTEREST. The unpaid principal amount of the Loan shall bear interest at a fixed rate of 5.00% per annum.

3. REPAYMENT. The entire unpaid principal amount of the Loan, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, shall be due and payable in 120 equal monthly installments, beginning on the first day of the first month following the expiration of a six (6) month grace period.

4. DEFERMENT. Borrower may defer payment of the principal and interest for a period of up to three (3) years, upon written request to Lender, if Borrower is enrolled in a full-time degree program at an accredited institution of higher learning.

5. FORBEARANCE. Upon written request, Lender may grant forbearance for a period not to exceed twelve (12) months, during which time the payment of the principal and interest shall be temporarily suspended.

6. SECURITY. The obligation of Borrower to repay the Loan shall be secured by a security interest in all of Borrower's right, title, and interest in and to the following property: a. All right, title, and interest in and to any and all personal property now owned
