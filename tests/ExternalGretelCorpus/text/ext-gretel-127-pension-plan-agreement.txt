**PENSION PLAN AGREEMENT**

**1. Introduction**

This Pension Plan Agreement (the "Agreement") sets forth the terms and conditions of the Defined Contribution pension plan (the "Plan") established and maintained by ABC Limited ("Employer") for the benefit of its eligible employees.

**2. Eligibility and Contributions**

2.1 Eligibility: Employer's full-time employees who are at least 18 years old and have completed one year of service are eligible to participate in the Plan.

2.2 Contributions: Both Employer and eligible employees are required to make contributions to the Plan. Employer's contributions will be a minimum of 3% of the employee's annual salary, while eligible employees are required to contribute a minimum of 5% of their annual salary. Contributions will be made on a pre-tax basis.

**3. Investment Options**

Participants may choose to invest their contributions among the following investment options:

3.1 Conservative Fund: A low-risk investment option that seeks to preserve capital and provide stable, modest returns.

3.2 Balanced Fund: A moderate-risk investment option that seeks to balance growth and income by investing in a mix of stocks and bonds.

3.3 Growth Fund: A higher-risk investment option that seeks to maximize long-term growth by investing primarily in stocks.

3.4 Socially Responsible Fund: An investment option that seeks to achieve long-term growth while aligning with socially responsible principles.

**4. Distribution Rules**

4.1 Distributions may be taken upon retirement, death, disability, or termination of employment.

4.2 Participants may elect to receive their distributions as a lump sum or as periodic payments over a specified period of time.

4.3 Distributions will be subject to applicable taxes and penalties, as required by law.

**5. Administration**

The Plan will be administered by a committee appointed by Employer (the "Committee"). The Committee will have the authority to interpret the terms of the Plan, resolve disputes, and make any other decisions necessary for the administration of the Plan.
