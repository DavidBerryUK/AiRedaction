Corporate Governance Guidelines

I. Introduction

These Corporate Governance Guidelines (the "Guidelines") establish a transparent and performance-based executive compensation framework for Alex Peron-Vasseur and other executives at the company. The Guidelines aim to align executive compensation with the company's long-term strategy and shareholder interests.

II. Fixed and Variable Components

The executive compensation framework includes a mix of fixed and variable components. The fixed component consists of a base salary, while the variable component is tied to the company's financial and operational performance. The variable component may include bonuses, stock options, and other performance-based incentives.

III. Performance Metrics

The company will establish clear and objective performance metrics for the variable component of executive compensation. These metrics may include revenue growth, profitability, shareholder returns, and other key performance indicators (KPIs) aligned with the company's long-term strategy.

IV. Clawback Provisions

The executive compensation framework incorporates clawback provisions to protect the company's interests. In the event of financial restatements or material violations of company policies, the company reserves the right to recover any incentive-based compensation paid to executives.

V. Disclosure and Accountability

The company will disclose executive compensation details in its annual reports to ensure accountability and alignment with shareholder interests. The disclosures will include information on base salaries, bonuses, stock options, and other forms of executive compensation.

VI. Review and Updates

The company will review and update these Guidelines periodically to ensure they remain relevant and effective. The Board of Directors, in consultation with the Compensation Committee, will oversee the review and update process.

She ensures that the framework is transparent, performance-based, and aligned with the company's long-term strategy. Macarena also collaborates with the Compensation Committee to review and update the Guidelines regularly, maintaining their relevance and effectiveness.
