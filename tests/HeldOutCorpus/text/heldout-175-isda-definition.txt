(a) "Benchmark" means any index, rate, price, value or other benchmark or determination (including any rate or determination based on a specified number of days' or weeks' interest or the time period between two dates) specified in the relevant Confirmation or, if not so specified, in the Schedule or, if not so specified in the Schedule, determined in accordance with any methodology or formula specified in the relevant Confirmation or, if not so specified, in the Schedule or, if not so specified in the Schedule, determined in accordance with any market convention or, if there is no market convention, determined in accordance with the determination procedures specified in the Schedule;

(b) "Benchmark Replacement" means, in relation to any Benchmark, the Benchmark Replacement specified in, or determined in accordance with, the Benchmark Replacement Provision;

(c) "Benchmark Replacement Provision" means the provision specified in the Schedule as being the Benchmark Replacement Provision for the relevant Benchmark;

(d) "Benchmark Transition Event" means, in relation to any Benchmark, the occurrence of any event or circumstance that triggers the operation of the Benchmark Replacement Provision for that Benchmark;

(f) "Relevant Market" means, in relation to any Benchmark, the market or markets conventionally taken to be the primary market or markets for transactions referencing that Benchmark;
