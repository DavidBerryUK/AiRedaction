NON-COMPETE AGREEMENT

This Non-Compete Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of this 1st day of March, 2023 (the "Effective Date"), by and between XYZ Corporation, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 500 Main Street, Anytown, USA ("Company"), and Marta Perales-Riera, residing at 015 Coleman Greens, 12024, Mccartyview ("Employee").

WHEREAS, Employee possesses certain confidential and proprietary information of Company; and

WHEREAS, Company desires to protect its confidential and proprietary information and maintain the goodwill of its business; and

WHEREAS, Employee is willing to agree not to compete with Company as provided herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. NON-COMPETE. Employee agrees that, during the term of this Agreement and for a period of twelve (12) months thereafter, Employee will not, directly or indirectly, whether as owner, partner, shareholder, director, employee, consultant, agent, or otherwise, engage in or carry on any business or activity that is competitive with the business of Company, in any geographic area where Company conducts its business.

2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION. Employee agrees to keep confidential and not to disclose or use, either during or after the term of this Agreement, any confidential or proprietary information of Company, including, but not limited to, customer lists, pricing policies, marketing strategies, trade secrets, and any other non-public information concerning the business or affairs of Company.

3. COMPENSATION. As consideration for the covenants and agreements made by Employee herein, Company shall pay Employee the sum of $______ upon execution of this Agreement.

4. TERM. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue in force for a period of three (3) years, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement.
