**Investment Prospectus**

**Introducing the Horizon Healthcare Innovation Fund**

The Horizon Healthcare Innovation Fund is a unique investment opportunity, focused on driving societal impact through targeted investments in healthcare companies. Our fund objectives, strategy, risks, and past performance are detailed below.

**Fund Objectives**

Our primary objective is to invest in healthcare companies that demonstrate the potential for significant societal impact. We seek to support businesses that align with our mission of improving health outcomes, enhancing access to healthcare services, and driving medical advancements.

**Investment Strategy**

We employ a rigorous, research-driven investment strategy, focusing on companies that possess a clear value proposition, a differentiated product or service, and a strong management team. Our investment approach includes:

1. Early-stage investments in innovative healthcare companies.
2. Growth-stage investments in companies with proven business models and scalable solutions.
3. Strategic partnerships with healthcare providers, research institutions, and other key stakeholders.

**Risks**

Investing in healthcare companies involves inherent risks, including market volatility, technological disruption, regulatory changes, and competitive pressures. We carefully evaluate each investment opportunity to mitigate these risks and optimize the potential for long-term value creation.

**Past Performance**

Historically, our investments have demonstrated strong performance, with a number of our portfolio companies achieving significant milestones, including:

- **Paulette C. Marchand**, CEO of MedHealth Innovations, successfully led the company through FDA approval for its groundbreaking telemedicine platform.
- **13813 Sims Village Suite 507**, the headquarters of Biosynth Labs, expanded its operations through a successful Series B funding round, enabling the company to scale its synthetic biology research.
