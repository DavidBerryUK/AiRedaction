Safety Data Sheet

SECTION 1: IDENTIFICATION

Product identifier: Hydrochloric Acid
Chemical formula: HCl
Recommended use: Laboratory use, cleaning solutions, pH adjustment

SECTION 2: HAZARD IDENTIFICATION

Classification: Corrosive, irritant
Hazard statement(s): Causes severe skin burns and eye damage. Harmful if swallowed.
Pictogram(s):
- Corrosive (GHS05)
- Health hazard (GHS07)
