**Ethics and Conduct Compliance Report**

**I. Executive Summary**

This Ethics and Conduct Compliance Report provides an overview of the ethics and conduct-related regulatory compliance efforts of XYZ Corporation for the fiscal year 2021. The report covers the audit findings, risk assessments, and remediation actions taken to ensure compliance with relevant financial regulations.

**II. Audit Findings**

The internal audit team conducted a comprehensive review of XYZ Corporation's ethics and conduct policies and procedures. The audit focused on the following key areas:

* Conflicts of interest
* Gifts and entertainment
* Political contributions
* Insider trading
* Whistleblower policies and procedures

The audit revealed that XYZ Corporation has implemented robust policies and procedures to address these key areas. However, the following areas of improvement were identified:

* Improved communication and training on the conflicts of interest policy
* Clarification of the gift and entertainment policy to ensure consistency in application
* Enhanced monitoring and reporting of political contributions

**III. Risk Assessments**

The risk assessment process involved identifying, analyzing, and prioritizing the ethics and conduct risks faced by XYZ Corporation. The following risks were identified as high priority:

* Reputational risk due to conflicts of interest
* Legal and regulatory risk due to insider trading
* Operational risk due to inadequate training and communication on ethics and conduct policies

**IV. Remediation Actions**

Based on the audit findings and risk assessments, the following remediation actions have been taken:

* Enhanced communication and training on the conflicts of interest policy, including the development of an online training module
* Clarification of the gift and entertainment policy and implementation of a regular review process to ensure consistency in application
* Implementation of a system to monitor and report political contributions
* Development of a communication plan to ensure all employees are aware of the whistleblower policies and procedures

**V. Conclusion**

XYZ Corporation is committed to maintaining the highest standards of ethics and conduct.
