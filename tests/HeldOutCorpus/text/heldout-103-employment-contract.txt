THIS AGREEMENT is made and entered into as of this 1st day of January, 19XX (the "Effective Date"), by and between ABC Corporation, a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), and John Doe, residing at 123 Main Street, Anytown, USA (the "Employee").

1. PURPOSE. The Company has granted the Employee the right to receive equity-based compensation as described in this Agreement.

2. DEFINITIONS.

2.1 “Award” means the equity-based award granted to the Employee pursuant to this Agreement.

2. VESTING.

3.1 The Award shall vest as follows:

(a) Twenty-five percent (25%) of the Award shall vest on the first anniversary of the Effective Date; and

(b) The remaining seventy-five percent (75%) of the Award shall vest in twenty-five percent (25%) increments on each anniversary of the Effect Tate thereafter, provided that the Employee remains employed by the Company through each such date.

3. TERMINATION.

3.1 In the event of termination of the Employee's employment for any reason other than Cause (as defined below), the unvested portion of the Award shall vest in full.

3.2 In the event of termination of the Employee's employment for Cause, the unvested portion of the Award shall be immediately forfeited.
