MARKETING AGREEMENT

This Marketing Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the date of acceptance by the Customer, by and between Chantal, a sole trader registered in England and Wales with company number 12345678, having its registered office at Abbas-Beckmann-Gasse 1, London, E1 4NS ("Marketing Partner"), and Harriet C. Wilson, a resident of [Customer Address] ("Customer").

WHEREAS, Marketing Partner is in the business of providing marketing services to businesses and individuals; and

WHEREAS, Customer desires to engage Marketing Partner to provide marketing services for its business, and Marketing Partner is willing to provide such services, subject to the terms and conditions set forth herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein, the parties hereto agree as follows:

1. MARKETING STRATEGIES

Marketing Partner will develop and implement marketing strategies to promote Customer's business, products, and services. Marketing Partner will use its best efforts to increase Customer's brand awareness, generate leads, and convert leads into customers. Marketing Partner will provide regular reports to Customer detailing the results of its marketing efforts.

2. BRAND USAGE RIGHTS

Customer hereby grants to Marketing Partner a non-exclusive, royalty-free, and fully paid-up license to use Customer's name, trademarks, logos, and other branding materials solely for the purpose of providing the marketing services described herein. Marketing Partner will not use Customer's branding materials for any other purpose without Customer's prior written consent.

3. PERFORMANCE EVALUATION

Customer will evaluate Marketing Partner's performance based on the following criteria:

* Increase in brand awareness
* Generation of leads
* Conversion of leads into customers
* Quality of marketing materials
* Timeliness of delivery of marketing materials
* Compliance with brand guidelines

4. TERM AND TERMINATION

This Agreement will commence on the date first set forth above and will continue for a term of twelve (12) months unless earlier terminated as provided herein. Either party may terminate this Agreement upon providing the other party with thirty (30) days' written notice.
