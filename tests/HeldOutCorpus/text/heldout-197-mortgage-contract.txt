**MORTGAGE CONTRACT**

This Mortgage Contract (the "Agreement"), dated as of the 1st day of August, 2021, is entered into between Elvira B. Jönsson, a resident of 395 Hans-J.-Hering-Platz, 82195, Tirschenreuth (the "Borrower"), and Apex Mortgage Lenders Ltd, a company organized and existing under the laws of the United Kingdom, with its head office located at 435 Oxford Street, London, W1C 2PE (the "Lender").

**1. LOAN APPROVAL AND SECURITY**

The Lender has approved the Borrower's application for a mortgage loan in the amount of £200,000 (the "Loan Amount"). The Borrower has agreed to secure the Loan Amount with the property located at 395 Hans-J.-Hering-Platz, 82195, Tirschenreuth (the "Property").

**2. REPAYMENT TERMS**

The Borrower shall repay the Loan Amount, together with interest, in equal monthly installments over a term of 30 years (the "Term"), according to the following schedule:

(a) For the first 15 years of the Term, the Borrower shall pay only the interest accruing on the Loan Amount at a fixed rate of 3.5% per annum (the "Interest-Only Period").

(b) After the Interest-Only Period, the Borrower shall pay both the interest and principal, at a fixed rate of 4.5% per annum, according to a standard amortization schedule (the "Amortization Period").

The Borrower acknowledges that, during the Interest-Only Period, the Borrower will not build any equity in the Property.

**3. INTEREST-FIRST STRUCTURE**

The Borrower understands and agrees that, during the Interest-Only Period, the monthly payments shall be applied first to the accrued interest and then, if any amount remains, to the reduction of the outstanding principal.
