Safety Data Sheet

Spill Response Procedure for Handling Chemicals at Logan-Nguyen

1. Purpose
The purpose of this Safety Data Sheet is to provide information on the properties of hazardous chemicals used at Logan-Nguyen, including physical, health, and environmental hazards, and to establish spill response procedures to contain and clean up chemical spills, minimizing environmental and health impacts.

2. Chemical Identity

* Chemical Name: Hydrochloric Acid
* Chemical Formula: HCl

3. Physical and Chemical Properties

* Appearance: Clear, colorless liquid
* Odor: Pungent, irritating
* Boiling Point: 110°C
* Melting Point: -85°C
* pH: 0-1
* Specific Gravity: 1.18 g/cm³

4. Fire-Fighting Measures

* Fire Extinguishing Media: Dry chemical, carbon dioxide, foam, or water spray when fighting an HCl fire.
* Special Hazards Arising from the Substance or its Container: Containers may rupture violently when exposed to heat or fire.

5. Accidental Release Measures

* Personal Protection: Wear appropriate personal protective equipment, including chemical-resistant gloves, safety goggles, and protective clothing.
* Evacuation: Evacuate the area and prevent entry.
* Spill Response: Contain and absorb the spill with inert materials, such as vermiculite, sand, or earth.
* Disposal Considerations: Dispose of in accordance with local, state, and federal regulations.

6. Handling and Storage

* Precautions for Safe Handling: Use non-sparking tools and equipment. Avoid contact with skin, eyes, and clothing.
* Conditions for Safe Storage: Store in a cool, well-ventilated area. Keep containers tightly closed.

7. Exposure Controls/Personal Protection

* Engineering Controls: Use local exhaust ventilation.
* Personal Protective Equipment: Use chemical-resistant gloves, safety goggles, and protective clothing.
