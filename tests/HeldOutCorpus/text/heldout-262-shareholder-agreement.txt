RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Corporation desires to set forth the rights and obligations of the Shareholder with respect to the securities of the Corporation owned by the Shareholder; and

The Corporation shall indemnify and hold harmless the Shareholder and its respective officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against any and all losses, claims, damages, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorney's fees) incurred by the Shareholder or its respective officers, directors, employees, and agents in connection with the purchase, ownership, or sale of the Shares or the performance of the Shareholder's duties hereunder, except to the extent such losses, claims, damages, liabilities, costs, and expenses are caused by the gross negligemce or willful misconduct of the Shareholder or its respective officers, directors, employees, and agents.

2. SHARES

The Corporation has issued and the Shareholder has purchased one hundred (100) shares of the Corporation's common stock, par value $0.001 per share (the "Shares").

3. VOTING RIGHTS

The Shareholder shall have the right to vote the Shares on all matters submitted to a vote of the Corporation's shareholders.

4. DIVIDENDS

The Shareholder shall be entitled to receive dividends on the Shares as and when declared by the Corporation's board of directors.
