THE PACIFIC COAST INSURANCE COMPANY

Insurance Policy
Policy Number: 2023-BP-12854

This insurance policy (the "Policy") is entered into between The Pacific Coast Insurance Company, a duly licensed insurance company incorporated under the laws of the state of California, with its principal place of business at 225 Main Street, San Francisco, CA 94105 (hereinafter referred to as the "Insurer"), and Leah Decker, the business owner of Decker's Designs, located at 17272 Nicholas Islands Apt. 681, CA 90210 (hereinafter referred to as the "Insured").

I. Coverage

This Policy provides coverage for business property, business interruption, and liability risks. The Insured's business property located at the aforementioned address, including but not limited to, buildings, equipment, and inventory, is covered against all risks of direct physical loss or damage, except as excluded herein. The Policy covers the Insured for loss of income resulting from suspension of the Insured's business operations due to direct physical loss or damage to the Insured's business property. The Policy also covers the Insured for liability risks, including but not limited to, bodily injury, property damage, personal injury, and advertising injury, arising out of the Insured's business operations, up to the policy limit.

II. Premiums

The premium for this Policy is $12,500, payable in monthly installments of $1,041.67. The premium is based on the Insured's business operations, location, and property values.
