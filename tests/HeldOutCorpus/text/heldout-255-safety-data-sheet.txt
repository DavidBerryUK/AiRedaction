### 3. Composition/information on ingredients

* **Substance Name:** High-Reach Cleaning Solution
* **Chemical Composition:** 80% Ethanol, 15% Water, 5% Surfactants

### 4. First-aid measures

* **In case of skin contact:** Wash off with soap and plenty of water.
* **In case of eye contact:** Rinse thoroughly with plenty of water for at least 15 minutes.
* **In case of ingestion:** Rinse mouth with water and do not induce vomiting. Seek medical advice immediately.

### 5. Fire-fighting measures

* **Extinguishing media:** Dry chemical, carbon dioxide, foam, or water spray when fighting an ethanol fire.
* **Special hazards arising from the substance or mixture:** Vapors may travel a considerable distance to a source of ignition and flash back.

### 6. Accidental release measures

* **Personal precautions:** Ensure good ventilation. Use personal protective equipment.
* **Environmental precautions:** Prevent entry into waterways, sewers, basements or confined areas.

### 7. Handling and storage

* **Precautions for safe handling:** Keep away from sources of ignition. Use non-sparking tools.
* **Conditions for safe storage:** Keep container tightly closed in a well-ventilated place.
