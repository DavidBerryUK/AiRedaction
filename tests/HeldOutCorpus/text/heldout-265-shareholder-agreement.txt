SHAREHOLDER AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT is made this ______ day of ________, 20_____, by and between ________________, a corporation organized and existing under the laws of the ________________, with its principal place of business located at ________________________ (hereinafter referred to as the "Corporation"), and ________________________, _______________________, and _______________________ (hereinafter referred to as the "Shareholders").

WHEREAS, the Corporation has authorized the issuance of ________ shares of its common stock, $_______ par value per share (the "Shares"); and

WHEREAS, the Shareholders have acquired or desire to acquire beneficial ownership of the Shares, and desire to set forth the terms and conditions of such ownership;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein, the parties hereto agree as follows:

1. ISSUANCE OF SHARES. The Corporation hereby agrees to issue and sell to each Shareholder the number of Shares set forth opposite such Shareholder's name on Schedule A attached hereto.

2. VOTING RIGHTS. Each Shareholder shall have the right to vote the Shares owned of record by such Shareholder on all matters submitted to a vote of the Shareholders.

3. DIVIDENDS. The Corporation shall declare and pay dividends on the Shares as and when declared by the Board of Directors.

4. TRANSFER RESTRICTIONS. The Shares may not be sold, transferred, pledged, hypothecated or otherwise disposed of, in whole or in part, except upon the prior written consent of the Board of Directors of the Corporation.

5. CONFLICT RESOLUTION.

(a) In the event of any dispute among the Shareholders arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the rights and obligations of the parties hereunder, the Shareholders shall first attempt in good faith to resolve such dispute through informal discussions.
