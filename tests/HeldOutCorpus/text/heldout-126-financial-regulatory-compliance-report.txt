**City National Bank**

Compliance Monitoring Report

January - December 2021

1. Introduction

City National Bank (CNB) is committed to maintaining a culture of compliance and adhering to all applicable financial regulations. This Compliance Monitoring Report summarizes the bank's compliance performance for the period of January - December 2021. The report includes audit findings, risk assessments, remediation actions, and recommendations for enhancing compliance monitoring processes.

2. Compliance Performance Metrics

During the reporting period, CNB's compliance performance metrics were as follows:

* Number of regulatory violations: 3
* Total fines and penalties: $50,000
* Number of employee training sessions conducted: 50
* Number of employees trained: 800
* Percentage of employees trained: 95%

3. Monitoring Results

The Compliance Monitoring Team conducted regular reviews of the bank's operations to ensure compliance with relevant regulations. Key findings include:

* Three instances of non-compliance with anti-money laundering (AML) regulations were identified and addressed through remediation actions.
* No instances of non-compliance with know-your-customer (KYC) regulations were identified.
* No instances of non-compliance with consumer protection regulations were identified.

4. Risk Assessments

The bank conducted regular risk assessments to identify and mitigate potential compliance risks. Key findings include:

* AML risk: Moderate
* KYC risk: Low
* Consumer protection risk: Low

5. Remediation Actions

The bank took the following remediation actions to address the identified instances of non-compliance:

* Implemented additional AML training for relevant staff.
* Updated AML policies and procedures.
* Conducted a review of all customer accounts to ensure compliance with AML regulations.
