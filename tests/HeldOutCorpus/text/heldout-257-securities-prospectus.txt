SECURITIES PROSPECTUS

I. ISSUING ENTITY

This securities prospectus is issued by GlobalTech PLC, a leading technology company headquartered in London, United Kingdom. GlobalTech PLC was established in 2005 and has since expanded its operations to over 30 countries worldwide. The company is primarily engaged in the research, development, and manufacturing of cutting-edge technology products for both consumer and enterprise markets.

II. TERMS OF THE OFFERING

GlobalTech PLC is pleased to announce the offering of Yankee Bonds to investors in the United States. The following terms and conditions apply:

1. Issue size: USD 1,000,000,000
2. Coupon rate: 4.5% per annum, payable semi-annually
3. Maturity: 10 years from the date of issuance
4. Issue price: 100% of face value
5. Denomination: USD 1,000
6. Listing: The bonds will be listed on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE)
7. Use of proceeds: The net proceeds from the offering will be used for general corporate purposes, including research and development, capital expenditures, and potential acquisitions.

III. RISK FACTORS

Investors should carefully consider the following risk factors before investing in the Yankee Bonds:

1. Credit risk: As a foreign entity, GlobalTech PLC is subject to credit risk, which refers to the possibility that the issuer may default on its debt obligations. Investors should assess the company's creditworthiness and financial health before investing.
2. Currency risk: The Yankee Bonds are denominated in U.S. dollars, and investors are exposed to currency risk due to fluctuations in the exchange rate between the U.S. dollar and the British pound. Investors should consider the potential impact of exchange rate movements on their investment returns.
3. Interest rate risk: The value of the Yankee Bonds may be affected by changes in market interest rates. If interest rates rise, the market value of the bonds may decline, and vice versa.
