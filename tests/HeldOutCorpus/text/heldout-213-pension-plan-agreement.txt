THE AMANDO VALENCIA SIMPLE IRA PLAN

This Pension Plan Agreement (the "Plan") is entered into as of January 1, 2023, by and between Amando Valencia, with a mailing address at 92453 Dunn Lights ("Participant"), and Ua12597-R ("Employer").

1. Purpose. The purpose of this Plan is to provide a Simplified Employee Pension Individual Retirement Account (a "SIMPLE IRA") for the benefit of Participant.

2. Contributions.

a. Employee Contributions. Participant may elect to defer up to $14,000 of compensation in 2023, or $17,000 if Participant is age 50 or older, to the Plan. Participant's elections shall be made on a salary reduction basis and shall be irrevocable for the calendar year.

b. Employer Contributions. Employer shall make either: (i) a matching contribution of up to 3% of Participant's compensation for the calendar year; or (ii) a nonelective contribution of 2% of Participant's compensation for the calendar year.

3. Investment Options. Participant may direct the investment of his or her account among various investment options offered by the SIMPLE IRA custodian.

4. Distributions.

a. General. Distributions from the Plan may be made only upon Participant's: (i) death; (ii) disability; (iii) attainment of age 59½; or (iv) severance from employment.

b. Early Withdrawal Penalty. If a distribution is made prior to Participant's attainment of age 59½, Participant may be subject to a 10% early withdrawal penalty imposed by the Internal Revenue Service.

5. Administration. This Plan shall be administered by Employer. Employer shall have the authority to interpret the Plan, to adopt rules and regulations for its administration, and to correct any errors or inconsistencies in the Plan.

6. Amendment and Termination. This Plan may be amended or terminated at any time by Employer upon written notice
