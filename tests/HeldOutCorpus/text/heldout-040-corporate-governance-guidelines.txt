Corporate Governance Guidelines

Introduction

At NFMUGBGD530, we are committed to conducting our business in accordance with the highest standards of ethical behavior and corporate governance. These Corporate Governance Guidelines set forth the principles and practices that define the structure and operation of our board of directors and management. This Code of Ethics reflects our company's values and provides clear guidelines for employee behavior.

Conflicts of Interest

Employees must avoid any actions or situations that could compromise their ability to make objective and fair decisions on behalf of the company. This includes avoiding any financial or other interests that could conflict with the interests of the company. If an employee becomes aware of a potential conflict of interest, they must disclose it to their manager or the compliance officer immediately.

Confidentiality

Employees must protect the confidentiality of all non-public information about the company, its clients, and its partners. This includes information about our financial performance, business strategies, and other sensitive information. Employees must not disclose confidential information to anyone outside the company, except as authorized by the company's policies and procedures.

Reporting Unethical Behavior

Employees have a responsibility to report any suspected violations of these Corporate Governance Guidelines or any other unethical behavior. Reports can be made anonymously through the company's whistleblower hotline or to the compliance officer. The company will investigate all reports promptly and take appropriate action to address any violations.

Any questions or concerns about these guidelines should be directed to Flor Miguela Barral or the compliance officer.
