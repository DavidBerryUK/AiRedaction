Media Compliance Guide: Regulatory Requirements and Standards

Introduction

This Media Compliance Guide outlines the regulatory requirements and standards that media companies must adhere to within their industry or jurisdiction. The guide covers media content regulation, advertising standards, and compliance with media industry regulations, including copyright laws and content licensing requirements.

Media Content Regulation

Media companies must ensure that their content complies with relevant regulations and standards. This includes adhering to broadcasting standards, such as those set by Ofcom in the UK, and the Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission (CRTC) in Canada. These standards cover issues such as violence, harm, and offense, as well as protecting the under-18 audience.

Advertising Standards

Media companies must also ensure that their advertising complies with relevant regulations and standards. This includes adhering to advertising codes of practice, such as those set by the Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) in the UK, and the Canadian Advertising Standards Council (CASC) in Canada. These codes cover issues such as misleading advertising, harm, and offense.

Copyright Laws and Content Licensing Requirements

Media companies must ensure that they comply with copyright laws and content licensing requirements. This includes obtaining the necessary licenses and permissions to use copyrighted material, and ensuring that they do not infringe on the rights of copyright owners.

For example, if a media company wants to use a clip from a movie or a song in their content, they must obtain the necessary licenses and permissions from the copyright owners. This includes obtaining a synchronization license to use the copyrighted material in conjunction with visual images, and obtaining a master use license to use the sound recording.

In addition, media companies must ensure that they comply with the terms of their content licenses. This includes adhering to any restrictions on the use of the licensed content, such as limitations on the duration of use, the territory of use, and the platforms on which the content can be distributed.
