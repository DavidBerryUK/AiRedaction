DISPUTE RESOLUTION POLICY

At Rent-a-Judge, we are committed to resolving disputes in a fair, efficient, and cost-effective manner. Our dispute resolution policy outlines the procedures and processes for resolving disputes between parties within our company or with external entities.

Rent-a-Judge offers a unique service that allows parties to hire a retired judge to oversee and help settle disputes through a private, flexible, and efficient process. Our retired judges have extensive experience in the legal field and are well-equipped to handle a wide range of disputes.

In the event of a dispute, the parties involved should first attempt to resolve the matter through informal discussions. If the dispute cannot be resolved through informal means, the parties may choose to participate in mediation. Mediation is a confidential and voluntary process in which a neutral third party, the mediator, helps the parties to communicate and negotiate a resolution.

If mediation is unsuccessful, the parties may choose to proceed to arbitration. Arbitration is a more formal process in which a retired judge, serving as the arbitrator, will hear evidence and arguments from both parties and make a binding decision. The arbitrator's decision is final and binding on the parties, and it may be enforced in the same manner as a court judgment.

In the event of a dispute, Rent-a-Judge will provide the parties with a list of retired judges who are available to serve as mediators or arbitrators. The parties may choose a mediator or arbitrator from this list, or they may choose to use a mediator or arbitrator of their own choosing.
