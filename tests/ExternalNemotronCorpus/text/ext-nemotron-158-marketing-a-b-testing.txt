### A/B Testing Report

#### Hypothesis
We hypothesize that changing the call-to-action (CTA) button color from blue to red on our landing page will increase the conversion rate by 15%.

#### Test Duration
The A/B test was conducted from 15/07/2026 to 30/07/2026.

#### Control and Variant Details

| Element            | Control                        | Variant                          |
|--------------------|--------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| CTA Button Color   | Blue                           | Red                              |
| Landing Page URL   | https://unbounce.com             | https://unbounce.com             |
| Target Audience    | USA                           | USA                             |

#### Traffic Allocation
Traffic was evenly split between the control and variant groups, with 50% of visitors directed to each version.

#### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI                | Control                        | Variant                          |
|--------------------|--------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Conversion Rate    | 2.5%                           | 3.7%                             |
| Revenue            | $5,000                         | $7,400                           |

#### Statistical Significance
The results were statistically significant at a 95% confidence level, indicating that the observed differences are unlikely due to random chance.

#### Results Interpretation
- The variant with the red CTA button showed a 1.2% increase in conversion rate compared to the control.
- Revenue generated by the variant was $2,400 higher than the control.
- These results suggest that the red CTA button is more effective in driving conversions and revenue.
