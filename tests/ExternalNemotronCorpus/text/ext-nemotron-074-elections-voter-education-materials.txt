# Voter Education Materials

## Voter Registration Guidelines

- **State**: Texas residents can register to vote online, by mail, or in person. Ensure you meet the state's requirements, including citizenship and age criteria.
- **Date**: The deadline to register to vote in the upcoming election is 11/15/2023.
- **Time**: The registration office closes at 17:45:30.

## Polling Place Information

- **County**: Webb County residents should visit their designated polling place. For more information, visit https://vote.gov/education/guide?type=pdf&language=en.
- **Coordinate**: The polling place is located at 30.2672 -97.7431.

## Sample Ballots

- Sample ballots will be available for review on the county's official website. Make sure to familiarize yourself with the candidates and issues before voting.

## Candidate Profiles

- **Race Ethnicity**: The candidates include individuals of Mexican descent.
- **Religious Belief**: Some candidates are Christian.
