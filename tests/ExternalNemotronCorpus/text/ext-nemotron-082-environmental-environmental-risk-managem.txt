VerdeVida, based in Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil, has developed an Environmental Risk Management Plan to address potential environmental hazards at our site located at coordinate 29.9758 S, 51.1341 W. This plan outlines strategies for risk identification, assessment, mitigation, and monitoring. Key components include regular environmental assessments, compliance with local regulatory standards, and implementation of mitigation measures such as waste management and pollution control. The plan also includes detailed maps and diagrams to illustrate risk areas and mitigation strategies.
