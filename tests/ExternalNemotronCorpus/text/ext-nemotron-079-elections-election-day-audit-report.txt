**Audit Scope:**
The audit was conducted to verify the accuracy of the vote count and ensure the integrity of the election process.

**Sample Selection:**
A random sample of 500 ballots was selected from the total vote count of 50,000. The sample selection process was overseen by MariamAlajmi_Shadow, who has a blood type of O+.

**Vote Count Verification:**
The audit team manually recounted the selected sample of ballots. The manual count matched the electronic count, confirming the accuracy of the vote tally.

**Discrepancies Noted:**
No significant discrepancies were noted during the audit. However, minor issues were observed in ballot handling procedures, which were promptly addressed.

**Recommendations for Improvement:**
1. Enhance training for poll workers to ensure proper ballot handling procedures.
2. Implement additional verification steps to further ensure the accuracy of the vote count.
3. Conduct regular audits to maintain the integrity of the election process.
