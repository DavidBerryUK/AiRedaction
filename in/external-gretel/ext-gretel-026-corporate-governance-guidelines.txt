Adams-Garcia Corporate Governance Guidelines: Health and Safety

1. Introduction

At Adams-Garcia, the safety and well-being of our employees, contractors, and visitors is of utmost importance. These Corporate Governance Guidelines outline our commitment to maintaining a secure work environment and providing comprehensive health and safety guidelines.

2. Risk Assessments

Regular risk assessments will be conducted to identify potential hazards and implement appropriate control measures. These assessments will cover all aspects of our operations, including but not limited to:

- Equipment and machinery
- Workstations and ergonomics
- Chemical and material handling
- Emergency response and evacuation procedures

3. Training and Education

All employees, contractors, and regular visitors will receive health and safety training relevant to their roles and responsibilities. This includes:

- Induction training for new hires, contractors, and visitors
- Periodic refresher training for existing personnel
- Specialized training for high-risk activities or positions

4. Reporting and Communication

Employees, contractors, and visitors are encouraged to report any hazards, incidents, or concerns related to health and safety. A confidential reporting system will be established to facilitate open communication and empower individuals to contribute to a safer work environment.

5. Continuous Improvement

Adams-Garcia is committed to continuously monitoring and improving safety performance. This includes:

- Regularly reviewing and updating our health and safety guidelines
- Implementing corrective actions based on incident investigations and audits
- Establishing performance metrics and targets to drive continuous improvement
