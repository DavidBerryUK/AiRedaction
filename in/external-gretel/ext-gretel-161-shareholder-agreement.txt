SHAREHOLDER AGREEMENT

This Shareholder Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of the date of acceptance by the shareholders, by and between Maximiliano Carvajal, with a mailing address of 264 Carpenter Lakes, 65805, New Kellyland (hereinafter referred to as "Shareholder"), and the company, [Company Name] (hereinafter referred to as the "Company").

WHEREAS, the Shareholder is the owner of [Number] shares of the Company's common stock; and

WHEREAS, the Company desires to set forth the rights and responsibilities of the Shareholder with respect to the appointment of board members;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein, the parties hereto agree as follows:

1. BOARD REPRESENTATION

1.1 Appointment of Board Members. The Shareholder shall have the right to appoint [Number] members to the Company's board of directors (the "Board"), provided that such appointments shall be subject to the approval of the Board.

1.2 Removal of Board Members. The Shareholder shall have the right to remove any board member appointed by the Shareholder, provided that such removal shall be subject to the approval of the Board.

1.3 Vacancies. In the event of a vacancy on the Board resulting from the death, resignation, or removal of a board member appointed by the Shareholder, the Shareholder shall have the right to appoint a replacement board member, subject to the approval of the Board.

2. VOTING RIGHTS

2.1 Voting. The Shareholder shall have the right to vote [Number] shares of the Company's common stock in accordance with the provisions of the Company's certificate of incorporation and bylaws.

2.2 Proxy. The Shareholder may appoint a proxy to vote the Shareholder's shares of the Company's common stock, provided that such appointment shall be in writing and shall be filed with the Secretary of the Company.
