REAL ESTATE LOAN AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT is made and entered into this 1st day of January, 2023, by and between ABC Limited, a company incorporated under the laws of Canada, having its registered office at 1234 Main Street, Toronto, Ontario (hereinafter referred to as "Lender"), and XYZ Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated under the laws of the United Kingdom, having its registered office at 5678 Park Lane, London (hereinafter referred to as "Borrower").

WHEREAS, Borrower desires to obtain financing from Lender for the acquisition and development of a parcel of land located at 999 Elm Street, Vancouver, British Columbia (hereinafter referred to as the "Property"); and

WHEREAS, Lender is willing to provide such financing on the terms and conditions set forth herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein, the parties hereto agree as follows:

1. LOAN AMOUNT: Lender shall provide to Borrower a loan in the amount of Five Million Dollars ($5,000,000.00) (the "Loan").

2. INTEREST RATE: The Loan shall bear interest at a rate of 6% per annum.

3. REPAYMENT TERM: The Loan shall be repayable in thirty-six (36) equal monthly installments commencing on the first day of the month next succeeding the date of this Agreement, and on the first day of each and every month thereafter until the Loan is fully repaid.

4. PROPERTY DETAILS: The Property is a parcel of land consisting of approximately 10 acres, zoned for mixed-use development, and located in an environmentally sensitive area. Borrower shall provide Lender with a detailed development plan for the Property, including a timeline for construction and a budget for the project.

5. SECURITY: The Loan shall be secured by a first mortgage on the Property.
