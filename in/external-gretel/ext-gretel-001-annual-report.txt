**Acme Corporation 2021 Diversity & Inclusion Annual Report**

**Introduction**

At Acme Corporation, we believe that a diverse and inclusive workforce is essential to our success. We are committed to fostering a culture that values and promotes diversity, equity, and inclusion at all levels of the organization. This report outlines our progress and initiatives in these areas over the past year.

**Demographics**

In 2021, our workforce consisted of:

* 55% women
* 45% men
* 12% underrepresented racial and ethnic minorities
* 4% individuals with disabilities
* 6% LGBTQ+ individuals

We are proud of the progress we have made in increasing the diversity of our workforce, but we recognize that there is still much work to be done.

**Recruitment and Hiring**

In 2021, we implemented several initiatives to increase the diversity of our candidate pool and reduce bias in the hiring process. These included:

* Partnering with diverse professional organizations and job boards
* Implementing a blind resume review process
* Providing unconscious bias training for all hiring managers

As a result, the percentage of underrepresented racial and ethnic minorities in our new hires increased by 50% compared to the previous year.

**Retention and Advancement**

We are committed to creating an inclusive culture where all employees feel valued and have the opportunity to advance in their careers. In 2021, we implemented several initiatives to support the retention and advancement of underrepresented groups, including:

* Establishing employee resource groups for women, racial and ethnic minorities, and LGBTQ+ individuals
* Providing leadership development programs specifically for underrepresented groups
* Implementing a mentorship program to support the career advancement of underrepresented employees

As a result, the percentage of underrepresented racial and ethnic minorities in leadership positions increased by 25% compared to the previous year.
