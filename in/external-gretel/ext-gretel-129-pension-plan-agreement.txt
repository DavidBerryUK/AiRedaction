**XYZ Organization 403(b) Pension Plan Agreement**

**1. Introduction**

This Agreement outlines the terms and conditions of the XYZ Organization 403(b) Pension Plan (the "Plan"), a tax-qualified retirement plan established under Section 403(b) of the Internal Revenue Code. The Plan is designed to provide eligible employees of XYZ Organization with a tax-advantaged method to save for retirement.

**2. Eligibility**

All employees of XYZ Organization who meet the eligibility requirements under Section 403(b) of the Internal Revenue Code are eligible to participate in the Plan.

**3. Contributions**

Eligible employees may make elective deferrals on a pre-tax basis, up to the annual maximum limit set by the Internal Revenue Service. XYZ Organization may also make employer matching contributions, subject to certain limitations and vesting requirements.

**4. Investment Choices**

Participants may choose from a range of investment options, including:

* Mutual funds
* Annuities
* Variable annuities
* Collective investment trusts

The investment options available under the Plan may change from time to time at the discretion of XYZ Organization.

**5. Distributions**

Distributions from the Plan may be made upon the occurrence of certain events, including:

* Retirement
* Death
* Disability
* Separation from service

Distributions may be taken as a lump sum or as periodic payments, subject to applicable rules and regulations. Distributions taken prior to age 59 1/2 may be subject to an additional 10% early withdrawal penalty.

**6. Administration**

The Plan is administered by XYZ Organization, which has the authority to interpret the terms of the Plan, make and amend rules and regulations, and resolve any disputes arising under the Plan.

**7. Amendment and Termination**

XYZ Organization reserves the right to amend or terminate the Plan at any time, subject to the requirements of applicable law.
