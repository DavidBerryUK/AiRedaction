THE DARIUSZ REUTER-SCHUSTER INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR SIMPLIFIED EMPLOYEE PENSION (SEP) PLAN AGREEMENT

1. ESTABLISHMENT OF PLAN

This Simplified Employee Pension (SEP) Plan (the "Plan") is hereby established and adopted by Dariusz Reuter-Schuster, an independent contractor ("Employer"), as of the date set forth below. The Employer hereby adopts this Plan for its taxable year 2022 and subsequent years.

2. EMPLOYER'S CONTRIBUTION REQUIREMENTS

The Employer agrees to make contributions to the Plan on behalf of eligible employees in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement and the applicable provisions of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended (the "Code"). The Employer's contributions shall be subject to the following limitations:

a. The Employer's contributions on behalf of each eligible employee shall not exceed the lesser of (i) 25% of the employee's compensation or (ii) $61,000 for taxable year 2022 (such amount to be adjusted for cost-of-living increases in subsequent years).

b. The Employer's contributions shall be made on a uniform basis for all eligible employees.

c. The Employer's contributions shall be made in cash and shall be deposited in a trust established for the exclusive benefit of the Plan's participants and their beneficiaries.

3. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA FOR EMPLOYEE PARTICIPATION

An employee shall be eligible to participate in the Plan if such employee:

a. Is 21 years of age or older;

b. Has performed services for the Employer during at least three of the immediately preceding five years; and

c. Has received at least $650 in compensation from the Employer during the year for which the contribution is being made.
