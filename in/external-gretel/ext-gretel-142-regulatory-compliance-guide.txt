Nonprofit Compliance Guide

1. Incorporation and Governance

1.1. Incorporation

* Incorporate your nonprofit organization in the jurisdiction where it will operate. This process typically involves filing articles of incorporation with the relevant state agency.

1.2. Bylaws

* Create a set of bylaws that outline the organization's purpose, governance structure, and operating procedures.

1.3. Board of Directors

* Appoint a board of directors responsible for overseeing the organization's activities and ensuring compliance with legal requirements.

1.4. Meetings and Minutes

* Hold regular board meetings and record minutes to document decision-making processes.

2. Federal Tax-Exempt Status

2.1. 501(c)(3) Status

* Apply for 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status from the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) to be exempt from federal income tax and eligible for tax-deductible donations.

2.2. Form 1023 or 1023-EZ

* File Form 1023 or 1023-EZ with the IRS to apply for tax-exempt status.

2.3. Public Charity Status

* Maintain public charity status by ensuring that more than one-third of your organization's financial support comes from the general public or from government agencies.

3. State and Local Compliance

3.1. State Registration

* Register your nonprofit organization with the relevant state agencies where it operates.

3.2. Charitable Solicitations Registration

* Register for charitable solicitations in states where you plan to fundraise. This process typically involves filing a registration form and paying a fee.

3.3. Fundraising Disclosures

* Disclose your organization's name, purpose, and registration information on all solicitation materials.

3.4. Financial Reporting

* File annual financial reports with the relevant state agencies.

4. Donor Transparency

4.1. Donor Privacy

* Protect donor privacy by keeping their personal information confidential, unless otherwise required by law.
