Financial Crime Compliance Report

Introduction

This Financial Crime Compliance Report has been prepared by the Compliance Department of Barclays Bank PLC in accordance with the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) regulations and guidelines. The report details the bank's measures for combating money laundering, terrorist financing, and other financial crimes. The report includes the results of the audit findings, risk assessments, and remediation actions taken during the period of April 1, 2021, to March 31, 2022.

Audit Findings

The audit was conducted by the Internal Audit Department of Barclays Bank PLC. The audit focused on the bank's compliance with the Money Laundering Regulations 2017, the Terrorism Act 2000, and the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002. The audit findings are as follows:

1. Customer Due Diligence (CDD) - The bank has implemented a robust CDD process. However, there were instances where the CDD process was not followed correctly. The bank has taken corrective actions to address this issue.
2. Suspicious Activity Reporting (SAR) - The bank has a well-established SAR process. However, there were delays in reporting suspicious activities in a few instances. The bank has taken corrective actions to address this issue.
3. Training and Awareness - The bank provides regular training and awareness programs to its employees. However, there were instances where employees were not aware of the latest regulations and guidelines. The bank has taken corrective actions to address this issue.

Risk Assessments

The bank has conducted a comprehensive risk assessment of its operations. The risk assessment identified the following key risks:

1. Geographical Risk - The bank has operations in high-risk jurisdictions. The bank has implemented enhanced due diligence measures for customers and transactions from these jurisdictions.
2. Customer Risk - The bank has customers who are engaged in high-risk activities. The bank has implemented enhanced due diligence measures for these customers.
3. Product and Service Risk - The bank offers products and services that are susceptible to financial crime. The bank has implemented enhanced due diligence measures for these products and services.
