THE RENTER'S INSURANCE POLICY

This Renter's Insurance Policy (the "Policy") is entered into by and between SureGuard Insurance Company, a legal entity duly incorporated and existing under the laws of the United States of America, hereinafter referred to as the "Insurer," and Nilo Barroso, a resident of 26572 Kyle Gardens, 69581, Benjaminland, hereinafter referred to as the "Policyholder," and provides coverage for the Policyholder's rental property located at the aforementioned address.

I. COVERAGE

This Policy provides coverage for the Policyholder's personal property, liability, and additional living expenses in the event of a covered loss. The coverage limits are as follows:

A. Personal Property Coverage: $30,000
B. Liability Coverage: $100,000
C. Additional Living Expenses: 30% of Personal Property Coverage

II. PREMIUMS

The Policyholder shall pay an annual premium of $300 for the coverage provided under this Policy.

III. POLICYHOLDER'S OBLIGATIONS

The Policyholder shall:

A. Provide true and accurate information to the Insurer;
B. Pay the premiums as they become due;
C. Cooperate with the Insurer in the investigation and defense of any claim or lawsuit;
D. Take reasonable steps to prevent further loss or damage.

IV. DATE OF BINDING

This Policy shall take effect on August 25, 2023, and shall continue in force for a period of one (1) year, unless otherwise terminated in accordance with the terms of this Policy.

V. TERRITORIAL LIMITATIONS

This Policy applies only to the rental property located at 26572 Kyle Gardens, 69581, Benjaminland, and shall not provide coverage for any property located outside of this address.

VI. ENDORSEMENTS

This Policy may be endorsed by the Insurer to add, delete, or change coverage, subject to the terms and conditions of this Policy.
