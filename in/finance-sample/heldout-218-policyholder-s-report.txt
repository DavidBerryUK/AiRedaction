Dear Mr. Pardo-Redondo,

At Richards and Sons, we are committed to providing our policyholders with transparent and comprehensive information about their insurance coverage. As part of this commitment, we have created this Insurance Glossary to help you better understand the terms and definitions related to your policy.

1. Premium: The amount of money that a policyholder pays to their insurance company for coverage. This can be paid in installments or as a lump sum.
2. Benefit: The amount of money that an insurance company pays to a policyholder or their beneficiary upon the occurrence of a covered event.
3. Deductible: The amount of money that a policyholder must pay out of pocket before their insurance coverage kicks in.
4. Coverage: The specific risks or events that an insurance policy covers.
5. Claim: A request made by a policyholder to their insurance company for payment of a benefit.
6. Policy: A contract between an insurance company and a policyholder that outlines the terms and conditions of their coverage.
7. Underwriting: The process by which an insurance company evaluates the risk associated with a potential policyholder and determines the appropriate premium to charge.
8. Actuary: A professional who uses statistical analysis to evaluate and manage risk for insurance companies.
9. Beneficiary: The person or entity designated to receive the benefits of an insurance policy in the event of the policyholder's death.
10. Insured: The person or entity covered by an insurance policy.
