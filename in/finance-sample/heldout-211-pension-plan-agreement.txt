2. CONSTITUTRE AND ADMINISTRATION

2.1 The Seanville Public Sector Employees' Retirement Plan is a Governmental Plan established and maintained for the exclusive benefit of its employees and their beneficiaries.

2.2 The Plan shall be administered by a Board of Trustees, which shall have the power and authority to control and manage the assets and operations of the Plan.

3. CONTRIBUTIONS

3.1 The Seanville Public Sector shall contribute an amount equal to 10% of the total compensation of each employee to the Plan on behalf of each employee.

3.2 Contributions shall be made on a regular basis, not less than quarterly, and shall be remitted to the Plan in accordance with the requirements of the Plan.

4. INVESTMENT OPTIONS

4.1 The Plan shall offer a range of investment options, including but not limited to, stocks, bonds, and mutual funds.

4.2 Each employee shall have the right to choose the investment options for their individual account.

5. BENEFIT FORMULA

5.1 The benefit formula for the Plan shall be as follows:

5.1.1 For each year of service, an employee shall be entitled to a benefit equal to 1.5% of their final average salary.

5.1.2 The benefit shall be calculated based on the employee's highest five years of compensation.
