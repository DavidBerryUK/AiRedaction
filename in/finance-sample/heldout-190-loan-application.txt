**Project Description:**
The Lake Cynthia Community Center aims to provide a central hub for community members to connect, learn, and grow. The center will offer various programs and activities for all ages, including after-school programs, adult education classes, and community events. The center will be located at the heart of Lake Cynthia, at the current vacant lot adjacent to the community park.

**Community Needs:**
The Lake Cynthia community has identified a need for a dedicated space for community members to gather and engage in activities that promote social, educational, and economic growth. The community center will address this need by providing a safe and inclusive space for all community members.

**Project Scope:**
The project involves the construction of a 10,000 square foot community center with a capacity of up to 200 people. The center will include a large multipurpose room, a kitchen, classrooms, offices, and restrooms. The project will also include the development of a small outdoor amphitheater for community events and gatherings.

**Anticipated Community Benefits:**
The Lake Cynthia Community Center will provide numerous benefits to the community, including:

* A central hub for community members to connect and engage in activities that promote social, educational, and economic growth.
* A safe and inclusive space for all community members.
* After-school programs for children and youth.
* Adult education classes.
