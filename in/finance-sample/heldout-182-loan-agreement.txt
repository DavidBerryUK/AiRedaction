SMALL AND MEDIUM-SIZED ENTERPRISE (SME) LOAN AGREEMENT

THIS LOAN AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is made and entered into as of this 1st day of January, 2023 (the "Effective Date"), by and between ABC Enterprises Ltd., a company organized and existing under the laws of Canada, with its head office located at 123 Main Street, Toronto, Ontario, Canada (the "Borrower"), and XYZ Bank, a chartered bank organized and existing under the laws of Canada, with its head office located at 456 Elm Street, Toronto, Ontario, Canada (the "Lender").

WHEREAS, the Borrower has requested that the Lender extend to it a loan in the principal amount of Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($500,000.00) (the "Loan") for the purpose of financing the Borrower's business operations and expansion; and

WHEREAS, the Lender is willing to extend the Loan to the Borrower on the terms and conditions set forth herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the parties hereto agree as follows:

1. LOAN

(a) Upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth herein, the Lender agrees to make the Loan to the Borrower in the principal amount of Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($500,000.00).

(b) The Loan shall be evidenced by a promissory note (the "Note") in the form attached hereto as Exhibit A.

2. REPAYMENT
