4. First-aid measures

In case of inadequate ventilation, provide fresh air. In case of skin contact, wash off with soap and plenty of water. In case of eye contact, rinse thoroughly with plenty of water for at least 15 minutes and consult a physician. In case of ingestion, seek medical advice immediately and show the container or the label.

5. Fire-fighting measures

Do not fight fires involving this material unless trained to do so. Use extinguishing agents suitable for surrounding fires. Do not allow fire to reach the containers. Cool containers with water spray if necessary.

6. Accidental release measures

In case of a spill, prevent entry into waterways, sewers, basements or confined areas. Stop leak if without risk. Absorb with inert material and place in containers for disposal.

7. Handling and storage

Keep container tightly closed. Store in a cool, well-ventilated area. Keep away from incompatible materials. Wash hands after handling. Use personal protective equipment, including gloves and eye protection.

8. Exposure controls/personal protection

Use appropriate personal protective equipment to prevent skin and eye contact. Use appropriate respiratory equipment if necessary.
