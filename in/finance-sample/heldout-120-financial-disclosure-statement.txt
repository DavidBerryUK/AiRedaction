FINANCIAL DISCLOSURE STATEMENT
Venture Capital Investment Profile

1. Introduction

This Financial Disclosure Statement outlines the venture capital investment activities of Pemberton Capital Partners LLP, a leading venture capital firm based in London, UK. The firm specializes in early-stage investments in technology, life sciences, and clean energy sectors.

2. Investment Portfolio

As of December 31, 2021, Pemberton Capital Partners LLP has invested in a diversified portfolio of 25 early-stage companies, with a total investment value of £125 million. The portfolio is allocated across the following sectors:

- Technology: 52%
- Life Sciences: 28%
- Clean Energy: 20%

3. Geographic Distribution

Pemberton Capital Partners LLP's investments are primarily focused in the following geographic regions:

- United Kingdom: 56%
- United States: 28%
- Europe: 16%

4. Board Representation

Pemberton Capital Partners LLP takes an active role in the companies it invests in, with board representation in 72% of its portfolio companies. The firm's partners have extensive experience in scaling businesses and provide strategic guidance to help drive growth.

5. Expected Returns

Pemberton Capital Partners LLP targets a net internal rate of return (IRR) of 25% on its investments. The expected holding period for investments is typically 5-7 years, with a focus on long-term value creation.

6. Exit Strategies

Pemberton Capital Partners LLP employs a variety of exit strategies, including:

- Strategic sales to industry leaders
- Initial Public Offerings (IPOs)
- Secondary sales to financial investors

The firm has a strong track record of successful exits, with an average multiple of invested capital (MOIC) of 3.2x.

7. Risk Management

Pemberton Capital Partners LLP has a robust risk management process in place, which includes thorough due diligence, diversification across sectors and geographies, and regular portfolio monitoring. The firm maintains a strong focus on capital preservation while pursuing attractive risk-adjusted returns.
