PENSION PLAN AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT is made this ____ day of _______, 20__, by and between the City of Springfield, a municipal corporation organized and existing under the laws of the State of Illinois (hereinafter “Employer”), and the Participants in the Springfield Municipal Employees' Retirement Plan (hereina Governmental Plan).

1. Purpose. The purpose of this Agreement is to establish the Springfield Municipal Employees' Reterms and conditions of the Plan.

2. Type of Plan. This is a Governmental Plan as defined in Section 414(d) of the Internal Rev Contributions.

3. Contributions.
a. Normal Cost. The annual normal cost shall be the actuarially determined cost of the benefits provided by the Plan, determined in accordance with the Actuarial Standards of Practice.
b. Employer Contributions. The Employer shall contribute on behalf of each Participant an amount equal to the normal cost.

4. Investment Options. Participants shall have the following investment options:
a. Stable Value Fund
b. Fixed Income Fund
c. Equity Index Fund
d. International Equity Fund
e. Balanced Fund

5. Benefits.
a. Normal Retirement. A Participant shall be entitled to a normal retirement benefit at age 65 if the Participant has five years of Service.
b. Early Retirement. A Participant may retire as early as age 55 with a reduced benefit if the Participant has five years of Service.
c. Disability Retirement. A Participant who becomes permanently and totally disabled may receive a disability retirement benefit.

6. Vesting. A Participant shall be 100% vested in the Participant'5.

7. Administration. The Plan shall be administered by a three-member Board of Trustees, consisting of two representatives of the City and one representative of the Participants.

8. Amendment and Termination. The City may amend or terminate the Plan at any time, subject to the provisions of ERISA.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the City has caused this Agreement to be executed by its authorized representative.
