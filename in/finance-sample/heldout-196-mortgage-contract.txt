MORTGAGE CONTRACT

This Mortgage Contract (the "Agreement"), dated as of the 1st day of January, 2023 (the "Effective Date"), is entered into between Yves Monique Guibert, a resident of Wilsonbury, with a mailing address of 226 Brittany Drive, 91541, Wilsonbury (hereinafter "Borrower"), and DFMVDEWF223, a mortgage lender (hereinafter "Lender").

WHEREAS, Borrower desires to borrow the sum of $500,000 (the "Loan Amount") from Lender, and Lender is willing to lend said amount to Borrower, subject to the terms and conditions set forth herein;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. LOAN TERMS

1.1 Loan Amount. Lender agrees to provide Borrower with a loan in the amount of $500,000 (the "Loan Amount").

1.2 Interest Rate. The interest rate for the Loan Amount shall be 4.00% per annum.

1.3 Repayment Term. The Loan Amount shall be repaid over a term of 5 years (the "Term"), with monthly payments due on the 1st day of each month.

1.4 Balloon Payment. At the end of the Term, Borrower shall make a balloon payment equal to the remaining outstanding balance of the Loan Amount.

2. COLLATERAL

As security for the Loan Amount and the timely payment of all amounts due hereunder, Borrower grants to Lender a first lien on the following property (the "Collateral"):

2.1 Real Property. The real property located at 226 Brittany Drive, 91541, Wilsonbury (the "Real Property").
