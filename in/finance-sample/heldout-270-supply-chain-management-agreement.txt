TRANSPORTATION COST ALLOCATION AGREEMENT

This Transportation Cost Allocation Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of this 1st day of January, 2023 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Anthony Robinson-Hunt, with a mailing address of 32934 Mariah Stravenue, Apt. 196, and the party listed on the signature page ("Company").

WHEREAS, Company and Anthony Robinson-Hunt desire to establish a fair and transparent method for allocating transportation costs among themselves;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein, the parties hereto agree as follows:

1. ALLOCATION OF TRANSPORTATION COSTS.

(a) Cost Sharing. The parties shall share equally in all transportation costs incurred in the performance of this Agreement.

(b) Fuel Surcharges. Any fuel surcharges imposed by a carrier shall be passed through to the other party on a dollar-for-dollar basis.

(c) Invoicing and Payment. Each party shall invoice the other party on a monthly basis for its share of transportation costs. Payment shall be due within thirty (30) days of receipt of the invoice.

(d) Audit Rights. Each party shall have the right to audit the other party's records related to transportation costs upon reasonable notice and during regular business hours.

2. TERM. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for a term of one (1) year, unless earlier terminated as provided herein.

3. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate this Agreement upon providing thirty (30) days' written notice to the other party.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each party agrees to keep confidential all non-public information received from the other party during the term of this Agreement.

5. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York.

6. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior negotiations, understandings and agreements between the parties, whether written or oral, relating to the subject matter hereof
