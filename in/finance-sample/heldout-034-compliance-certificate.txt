Cybersecurity Compliance Assessment Certificate

This is to certify that Sjödin & Andersson HB has undergone a comprehensive cybersecurity compliance assessment and has been found to be in compliance with all relevant industry standards and regulations.

Assessment was conducted in accordance with internationally recognized best practices, including but not limited to, the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Cybersecurity Framework and the ISO/IEC 27001:2013 standard for Information Security Management Systems.

The assessment included a thorough review of Sjödin & Andersson HB's information security policies, procedures, and controls. Key areas of focus included:

- Access control and authentication
- Incident management and response
- Data protection and privacy
- Vendor management
- Physical and environmental security

Sjödin & Andersson HB has demonstrated a strong commitment to maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of its information assets. Specifically, the following controls were found to be in place and operating effectively:

- Multi-factor authentication for remote access
- Regular vulnerability assessments and penetration testing
- Encryption of sensitive data in transit and at rest
- Segregation of duties and least privilege principle
- Regular backups and offsite storage
- Incident response plan and regular testing

It was also noted that Sjödin & Andersson HB has appointed a Data Protection Officer and has implemented a comprehensive data protection and privacy program, including regular employee training and awareness programs.

Furthermore, Sjödin & Andersson HB has provided evidence of compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS).

Based on the results of the assessment, it is my professional opinion that Sjödin & Andersson HB has met the applicable cybersecurity compliance requirements.

Certification is valid from the date of this report until the next annual assessment, unless otherwise noted.
