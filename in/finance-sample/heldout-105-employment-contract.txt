SEASONAL EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Seasonal Employment Agreement (the "Agreement") is made and entered into on this 2nd day of February, 2002, by and between XYZ Corporation, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 123 Main Street, Anytown, USA ("Company"), and Danielle A. Mendès, residing at 7730 Bethany Dam, Apt. 7119, Anytown, USA ("Employee").

1. Employment. The Company hereby employs Employee as a seasonal employee for the period commencing on February 15, 2023 and ending on January 10, 2024 (the "Term"). Employee shall perform such duties as are assigned to her by the Company from time to time, consistent with her position and qualifications.

2. Compensation. The Company shall pay Employee a salary of $20.00 per hour for all hours worked during the Term. Employee shall be eligible for overtime pay at a rate of one and one-half times her regular hourly rate for all hours worked in excess of forty (40) hours in any workweek.

3. Benefits. Employee shall not be eligible for any benefits, including but not limited to, health insurance, retirement plans, or paid time off, during the Term.

4. Termination. The Company may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, upon providing Employee with written notice. Employee may terminate this Agreement upon providing the Company with at least two (2) weeks' written notice.

5. Confidentiality. Employee agrees to keep confidential all non-public information concerning the Company that she learns during the course of her employment, and to return all such information to the Company upon termination of this Agreement.

6. Miscellaneous. This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior negotiations, understandings, and agreements between them. This Agreement may not be amended or modified except in writing signed by both parties.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first above written.
