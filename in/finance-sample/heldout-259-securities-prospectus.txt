SECURITIES PROSPECTUS

ISIN: US1234567890

ISSUER: ABC Corporation

This Securings Prospectus relates to the proposed issue by ABC Corporation (the “Issuer” or “ABC Corp.”) of $500,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 7.50% Senior Notes due 2030 (the “Notes”). The Notes will be unsecured obligations of the Issuer and will rank pari passu with all other unsecured and unsubordinated obligations of the Issuer.

The Issuer is a leading global manufacturer of specialized industrial equipment, with a strong market position in its core markets and a diversified customer base. The company has a strong track record of financial performance, having consistently delivered strong revenue growth and strong profitability over the past five years.

The following is a summary of key terms of the proposed offering:

* **Issuer*: ABC Corporation
* **Issue Date*: March 1, 3023
* **Maturity Date*: March 1, 2030
* **Principal Amount*: $500,000,400
* **Coupon*: 7.50% per annum
* **Interest Payment Dates*: Semiannually, on March 1 and September 1, commencing September 1, 2023
* **Denominations*: $2,000
* **Covenants*: The Notes will be subject to certain covenants, including, but not limited to, restrictions on the issuance of additional debt, the creation of liens, and the payment of dividends
* **Transfer Restrictions*: The Notes will be subject to restrictions on transfer
* **Use of Proceeds*: The net proceeds from the sale of the Notes will be used for general corporate purposes, including the repayment of debt and the financing of capital expendit
