BOAT INSURANCE POLICY

This Boat Insurance Policy (the "Policy") is entered into by and between SureSafe Marine Insurance Company, a legal entity duly incorporated and existing under the laws of the state of Delaware, with its principal place of business at 1234 Fifth Avenue, Wilmington, DE 19801, hereinafter referred to as the "Insurer," and Jörn Lucia Eimer, residing at 034 Cervantes Locks, Apt. 409, hereinafter referred to as the "Insured" or "Policyholder."

I. INSURANCE COVERAGE

This Policy provides coverage for the following:

A. Physical Damage Coverage

The Insurer agrees to pay for direct physical loss or damage to the described boat, including its machinery, equipment, and permanently attached accessories, caused by or resulting from the following:

1. Fire, lightning, explosion, theft, vandalism, or malicious mischief;
2. Windstorm, hail, or water damage (including sinking, stranding, or collision);
3. Contact with any submerged object or artificial object (except when operating in fresh water).

B. Liability Coverage

The Insurer will pay on behalf of the Insured all sums which the Insured shall become legally obligated to pay as damages because of:

1. Bodily injury or death of any person;
2. Property damage to property of others;

Arising out of the ownership, operation, or use of the described boat, subject to the terms, conditions, and limitations of this Policy.

C. Medical Payments Coverage

The Insurer will pay all reasonable medical expenses incurred by any person while occupying the described boat, subject to the terms, conditions, and limitations of this Policy.

D. Optional Equipment Coverage

The Insurer will pay for direct physical loss or damage to the following optional equipment:

1. Portable radios, televisions, CD players, and other electronic equipment not permanently installed in the boat;
2. Fishing gear, including rods, reels, tackle, and trolling motors;
3. Water skis, inflatable devices, and other similar recreational equipment.
