SECURITIES PROSPECTUS

I. INTRODUCTION

This Securities Prospectus relates to the offer for sale of distressed debt securities issued by Beta Limited (the "Issuer"). The Issuer is a UK-based manufacturing company that has been experiencing financial difficulties due to a combination of market conditions and internal management issues.

II. TERMS OF THE OFFERING

The offering consists of £50,000,000 in face value of the Issuer's distressed debt securities (the "Securities"). The Securities will be sold at a discount to their face value, with the initial offering price set at £35,000,000. The Securities will bear interest at a rate of 10% per annum, payable quarterly in arrears. The Securities will mature in five years from the date of issuance.

III. RISK FACTORS

Investing in distressed debt securities is inherently risky and prospective investors should carefully consider the following risk factors before making an investment decision:

* The Issuer is currently experiencing financial difficulties and there can be no assurance that the Issuer will be able to meet its obligations under the Securities.
* The market value of the Securities may be volatile and may be influenced by a number of factors, including the financial condition of the Issuer, market conditions, and general economic conditions.
* The recovery prospects for the Securities are uncertain and may be influenced by a number of factors, including the financial condition of the Issuer, the outcome of any legal proceedings involving the Issuer, and market conditions.
* The legal and financial strategies employed in distressed debt investing can be complex and may involve a high degree of risk. Prospective investors should carefully consider whether such strategies are suitable for their investment objectives and risk tolerance.

IV. USE OF PROCEEDS

The net proceeds from the sale of the Securities will be used by the Issuer for general corporate purposes, including working capital, debt repayment, and capital expenditures.
