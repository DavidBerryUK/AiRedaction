PRIVATE PLACEMENT BOND OFFERING

This Prospectus relates to the offer and sale of securities by Costanzo R. Lollobrigida (the "Issuer") pursuant to an exemption from the registration requirements of applicable securities laws. The securities are being offered and sold solely to institutional investors that are qualified purchasers or accredited investors (as such terms are defined in Rule 144A and/or Regulation D promulgated under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the "Securities Act")) in reliance on exemptions from the registration requirements of the Securities Act and any applicable state securities laws. The securities will not be registered under the Securities Act or any state securities laws and, unless so registered, may not be offered or sold in the United States except pursuant to an exemption from, or in a transaction not subject to, the registration requirements of the Securities Act and applicable state securities laws.

The Issuer is a privately held company organized under the laws of the state of Delaware, with its principal place of business located at 428 Stephanie Crossing, Apt. 33831, [City], DE 19999.

The Issuer is issuing up to $50,000,000 in aggregate principal amount of Private Placement Bonds (the "Bonds"). The Bonds will bear interest at a rate of 5.00% per annum, payable semi-annually in arrears on June 30 and December 31 of each year, commencing on December 31, 2022. The Bonds will mature on December 31, 2032. The Bonds will be issued in denominations of $1,000 and integral multiples thereof.

The Bonds will not be subject to any sinking fund or redemption provisions. The Bonds will rank pari passu in right of payment with all other unsecured and unsubordinated indebtedness of the Issuer.

The Issuer intends to use the net proceeds from the sale of the Bonds for general corporate purposes, including working capital, capital expenditures, and potential acquisitions.
