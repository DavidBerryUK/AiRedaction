FLOOD INSURANCE POLICY

This Flood Insurance Policy (the "Policy") is entered into as of this 1st day of March, 2023, between National Flood Insurance Program ("NFIP") and the insured person named herein.

I. INSURED PERSON

The insured person under this Policy is Virginie Flavia Elvira Zacco, with a mailing address of 349 Scott Plaza Suite 058.

II. PROPERTY COVERED

The property covered by this Policy is a residential building located at the above-mentioned address.

III. COVERAGE LIMITS

The NFIP will pay up to $250,000 for damage to the insured building and up to $100,000 for damage to the insured personal property.

IV. PREMIUM AMOUNTS

The annual premium for this Policy is $450.

V. POLICY ENDORSEMENTS

This Policy includes the following endorsements:

* Increased Cost of Compliance Coverage
* Contents Replacement Cost Coverage
* Other Structures Coverage

VI. DEDUCTIBLE

The deductible for this Policy is $1,000.

VII. TERM OF THE POLICY

This Policy will remain in force for one year from the effective date and will automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless terminated by either party.

VIII. CANCELLATION

Either party may cancel this Policy at any time by giving written notice to the other party.

IX. NOTICE OF CLAIM

The insured must give notice to the NFIP as soon as practicable after a loss covered by this Policy occurs.

X. GOVERNING LAW

This Policy will be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the jurisdiction in which the property is located.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the NFIP has caused this Policy to be executed by its duly authorized representative.
