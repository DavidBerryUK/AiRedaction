SUPPLY CHAIN MANAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Supply Chain Management Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of this 1st day of January, 2023 (the "Effective Date") by and between Zoe's Supply Chain Solutions Inc., a corporation organized and existing under the laws of the Province of Ontario, with its head office located at 3032 Travis Junctions, Toronto, Ontario, Canada ("Zoe's"), and Jennifer Coca, a resident of the United Kingdom, with her mailing address at 45 King's Cross, London, UK ("Client").

WHEREAS, Zoe's is in the business of providing quality assurance and supply chain management services to its clients; and

WHEREAS, Client desires to engage Zoe's to provide quality assurance services in connection with Client's supply chain, and Zoe's is willing to provide such services, subject to the terms and conditions set forth herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein, the parties hereto agree as follows:

1. Scope of Services. Zoe's shall provide quality assurance services to Client in connection with Client's supply chain (the "Services"). The Services shall include, but not be limited to, product inspections, quality control processes, and performance benchmarks for maintaining product quality.

2. Performance Standards. Zoe's shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner in accordance with industry standards. Zoe's shall use commercially reasonable efforts to meet the following performance standards:

(a) Inspection Accuracy. Zoe's shall achieve an accuracy rate of 95% or higher in all product inspections.

(b) On-Time Delivery. Zoe's shall deliver all inspection reports and other deliverables within three (3) business days of the inspection.

(c) Quality Control. Zoe's shall maintain a quality control process that meets or exceeds industry standards.

3. Fees and Payment. Client shall pay Zoe's a fee of $5,000 per month for the Services. The fee shall be paid in advance on the first day of each month.
