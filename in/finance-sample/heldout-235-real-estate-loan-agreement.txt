**STUDENT HOUSING DEVELOPMENT LOAN AGREEMENT**

THIS AGREEMENT is made this ______ day of ________, 20______, by and between _______________ Bank, a banking corporation organized and existing under the laws of the United Kingdom, with its head office located at 171.171.77.5 (hereinafter referred to as "Lender"), and Cassandra Susanna Saragat, an individual with a residential address at 20 Erna-Rohleder-Allee, Mayen (hereinafter referred to as "Borrower").

WHEREAS, Borrower desires to obtain financing from Lender for the construction and development of a student housing project; and

WHEREAS, Lender is willing to provide such financing on the terms and conditions set forth herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the parties hereto agree as follows:

1. **LOAN AMOUNT:** Lender shall provide to Borrower a loan in the amount of £5,000,000 (the "Loan Amount") for the purpose of financing the construction and development of a student housing project (the "Property") located at a university campus near Mayen, Germany.

2. **INTEREST RATE:** The Loan Amount shall bear interest at a rate of 4.5% per annum.

3. **REPAYMENT SCHEDULE:** The Loan Amount shall be repaid in equal monthly installments over a term of 10 years, with the first installment due on the first day of the month next succeeding the date of this Agreement.

4. **PROPERTY DETAILS:** The Property shall consist of a state-of-the-art student housing facility, comprising of 200 units, with a mix of studio, one-bedroom, and two-bedroom apartments. The Property shall be equipped with modern amenities, including a fitness center, study rooms, a game room, and high-speed internet access. The Property shall be located within walking distance of the university campus and shall target a student population demographic of postgraduate students
