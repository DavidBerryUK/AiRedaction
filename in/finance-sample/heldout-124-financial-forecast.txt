**Cost-Benefit Analysis: Investment in Automated Manufacturing Equipment**

The management of XYZ Manufacturing Ltd. is considering an investment in automated manufacturing equipment to upgrade their current production line. This Cost-Benefit Analysis provides a financial forecast to evaluate the feasibility of this investment.

*Costs*

1. Equipment Purchase: The cost of purchasing and installing the new automated manufacturing equipment is estimated to be £2,500,000.
2. Training: Employee training on the new equipment will cost approximately £150,000.
3. Maintenance: Annual maintenance costs for the new equipment are estimated to be £50,000.
4. Downtime: The implementation project will result in 2 weeks of production downtime, costing an estimated £200,000 in lost revenue.

*Benefits*

1. Increased Production: The new equipment is expected to increase production capacity by 30%, resulting in additional annual revenue of £1,800,000.
2. Labor Cost Savings: The new equipment will reduce the need for manual labor, saving an estimated £720,000 annually in labor costs.
3. Quality Improvement: The automated equipment is expected to improve product quality, reducing defects by 50%. This could result in a decrease of £100,000 in warranty and repair costs.
4. Intangible Benefits: The investment in automated equipment is expected to enhance the company's reputation, potentially leading to new business opportunities. While difficult to quantify, this could result in significant additional revenue.

*Financial Metrics*

1. Net Present Value (NPV): Using a discount rate of 10%, the NPV of the project is estimated to be £2,147,367.
2. Internal Rate of Return (IRR): The IRR of the project is estimated to be 28%.
3. Payback Period: The payback period for the initial investment is approximately 2.3 years.
