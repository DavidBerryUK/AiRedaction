PRODUCT DISCLOSURE STATEMENT

ALTERNATIVE INVESTMENT DISCLOSURE

This Product Disclosure Statement (PDS) provides important information about the alternative investment opportunities offered by Luxor Capital Management Ltd. (Luxor). This PDS is designed to help you understand the features, risks, and costs associated with investing in our alternative investment strategies, including hedge funds, private equity, venture capital, and real assets. By reading this PDS, you will be better equipped to make informed investment decisions.

1. Investment Strategies

Luxor offers a range of alternative investment strategies designed to provide investors with diversification, capital appreciation, and income. Our strategies include:

1.1 Hedge Funds
Our hedge funds employ a variety of investment styles, such as long/short equity, event-driven, global macro, and relative value. These funds seek to generate absolute returns by investing in a broad range of asset classes, including equities, fixed income, currencies, and commodities.

1.2 Private Equity
Our private equity investments focus on acquiring and managing private companies, with the goal of creating value through operational improvements, strategic repositioning, and financial restructuring. We target investments in various sectors, including technology, healthcare, consumer, and industrials.

1.3 Venture Capital
Our venture capital investments focus on early-stage and growth-stage companies with innovative products, services, or business models. We seek to identify and invest in disruptive technologies and companies with high growth potential.

1.4 Real Assets
Our real assets strategy focuses on investing in tangible assets, such as real estate, infrastructure, and natural resources. We aim to generate income and capital appreciation by investing in assets that provide essential services or are backed by long-term contracts.

2. Fund Structures

Luxor's alternative investment funds are typically structured as limited partnerships, with Luxor acting as the general partner and investors acting as limited partners. Limited partners contribute capital to the partnership and share in the profits and losses of the partnership. Limited partners have limited liability, meaning their personal assets are not at risk in the event of partnership losses.
