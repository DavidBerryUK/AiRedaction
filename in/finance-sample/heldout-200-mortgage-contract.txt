MORTGAGE CONTRACT

THIS AGREEMENT is made this ______ day of ________, 20__, by and between _______________ (hereinafter "Lender") and _______________ (hereinafter "Borrower").

WHEREAS, Borrower desires to obtain a mortgage loan from Lender to purchase or refinance real property located at _______________________ (the "Property"); and

WHEREAS, Lender is willing to make such loan to Borrower on the terms and conditions set forth herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. LOAN AMOUNT. Lender agrees to loan to Borrower the sum of ________________ Dollars ($______), which sum shall be disbursed to Borrower or on Borrower's behalf in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

2. INTEREST RATE. The interest rate on the loan shall be ________ percent (_______%) per annum, adjustable annually on the anniversary date of the loan. The initial interest rate shall be in effect for the first ________ years of the loan term. Thereafter, the interest rate shall be adjusted annually based on the then-current one-year London Interbank Offered Rate (LIBOR) plus a margin of ________ percent (_______%). The initial interest rate shall be ________ percent (_______%).

3. REPAYMENT TERMS. The loan shall be repaid in equal monthly installments over a term of ________ years, commencing on the first day of the month next succeeding the date of disbursement of the loan proceeds. Each monthly installment shall consist of principal and interest, calculated on the outstanding principal balance at the then-current interest rate.
