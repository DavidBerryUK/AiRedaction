**Product Disclosure Statement**

Investment in the technology sector can be a lucrative opportunity, but it is essential to understand the features, risks, and costs involved. This Product Disclosure Statement provides detailed information about our Technology Investment Disclosure, a financial product designed to help you make informed investment decisions.

**Investment Strategy**

Our Technology Investment Disclosure focuses on software companies and emerging technologies that are poised to disrupt the market. We utilize a data-driven approach to identify innovation-driven investment opportunities. Our team of experts conducts thorough research and analysis to ensure that the investments align with market trends and have the potential for long-term growth.

**Features**

Our Technology Investment Disclosure offers the following features:

* Diversification: Our investment strategy is designed to provide a diversified portfolio, reducing the risk associated with investing in a single technology.
* Transparency: We provide regular updates on the performance of the investments, allowing you to monitor the progress of your investment.
* Flexibility: Our investment strategy is flexible and can be tailored to meet your specific financial goals and risk tolerance.

**Risks**

Investing in technology comes with its own set of risks, including:

* Market Risk: The value of your investment may fluctuate due to changes in the technology market.
* Technological Obsolescence: The technology or software company you invest in may become obsolete, leading to a decrease in the value of your investment.
* Regulatory Risk: Changes in regulations or legislation may impact the profitability of the technology or software company you invest in.

**Costs**

The following costs are associated with our Technology Investment Disclosure:

* Management Fees: We charge an annual management fee of 1.5% of the total value of your investment.
* Performance Fees: We charge a performance fee of 20% of the annual return on your investment, subject to a high-water mark.
