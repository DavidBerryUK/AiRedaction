**THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT EMPLOYEES' PENSION SCHEME**

**1. INTRODUCTION**

1.1 This document outlines the terms and conditions of the Local Government Employees' Pension Scheme (the "Scheme"), a Governmental Plan established and maintained by the Local Government Pension Committee (the "Committee") for the benefit of eligible employees of participating local authorities.

1.2 The Scheme is a funded defined benefit pension scheme, providing retirement benefits to eligible employees in accordance with the provisions of the Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 2013 and other relevant legislation.

**2. CONTRIBUTION REQUIREMENTS**

2.1 Members of the Scheme are required to contribute a percentage of their pensionable pay, as determined by the Committee from time to time, in accordance with the Scheme's funding requirements and the applicable legislation.

2.2 Employers are required to contribute a percentage of their employees' pensionable pay, as determined by the Committee from time to time, in accordance with the Scheme's funding requirements and the applicable legislation.

**3. INVESTMENT OPTIONS**

3.1 The Committee has delegated the management of the Scheme's assets to the Local Government Pension Scheme Advisory Board (the "Board"), which is responsible for investing the Scheme's assets in accordance with the Board's Statement of Investment Principles and the requirements of the Trustee Act 2000.

3.2 Members do not have the option to choose their own investment options, but the Board will ensure that the Scheme's investments are managed in accordance with the principles of diversification, risk management, and long-term growth.

**4. DISTRIBUTION RULES**

4.1 Members will be entitled to receive benefits from the Scheme upon reaching the normal pension age, as determined by the applicable legislation, or in accordance with the Scheme's early retirement provisions.

4.2 Benefits will be calculated in accordance with the Scheme's benefit formulas, which take into account the member's pensionable service, final pensionable pay, and the accrual rate specified in the applicable legislation.
