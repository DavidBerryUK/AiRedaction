**Fitness and Wellness Program**

**Program Overview**
The Fitness and Wellness Program is designed to promote overall health and well-being through structured physical activities and nutritional guidance. The program will run from 2023-09-15 and will be conducted by certified fitness professionals.

**Goals and Objectives**
- Improve cardiovascular health through regular cardio exercises.
- Enhance muscle strength and endurance through strength training.
- Promote flexibility and stress relief through yoga sessions.
- Educate participants on proper nutrition to support their fitness goals.
- Encourage a healthy lifestyle among participants.

**Target Audience**
This program is open to individuals of various fitness levels, including those who are Somerset and have an education level of 9th to 12th grade, no diploma. The program aims to cater to a diverse group of individuals, including clinical laboratory technologists or technicians.

**Schedule**
The program will follow a weekly schedule with designated days for different activities. Participants can refer to the calendar below for the detailed schedule:

| Day       | Activity          |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Monday    | Cardio            |
| Tuesday   | Strength Training |
| Wednesday | Yoga              |
| Thursday  | Nutrition Workshop|
| Friday    | Cardio            |
| Saturday  | Strength Training |
| Sunday    | Rest Day          |

**Activities**
- **Cardio**: Includes activities such as running, cycling, and aerobics.
- **Strength Training**: Focuses on weightlifting and resistance exercises.
- **Yoga**: Incorporates various yoga poses to improve flexibility and reduce stress.
- **Nutrition**: Workshops and consultations to educate participants on healthy eating habits.

**Instructors**
- **Cardio and Strength Training**: Certified fitness instructors.
- **Yoga**: Certified yoga instructors.
- **Nutrition**: Registered dietitians.

**Equipment Needed**
- **Cardio**: Treadmills, stationary bikes, and jump ropes.
- **Strength Training**: Dumbbells, barbells, and resistance bands.
- **Yoga**: Yoga mats and blocks.
- **Nutrition**: Educational materials and sample foods.

**Safety Guidelines**
- Always warm up before starting any exercise.
- Stay hydrated throughout the workout.
- Use proper form and technique during strength training.
- Listen to your body and avoid overexertion.
- Consult with a healthcare provider before starting any new fitness program.

**Evaluation Criteria**
Participants will be evaluated based on their progress in the following areas:
- **Cardiovascular Endurance**: Improvement in cardio performance.
- **Muscle Strength**: Increase in strength and endurance.
- **Flexibility**: Improvement in range of motion.
- **Nutrition Knowledge**: Understanding of healthy eating habits.
- **Overall Wellness**: General improvement in health and well-being.
