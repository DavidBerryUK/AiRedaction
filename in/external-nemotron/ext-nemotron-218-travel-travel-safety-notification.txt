**Travel Safety Notification**

We are issuing this urgent travel advisory for the USA. The alert level has been raised to high due to recent events. Travelers are advised to exercise increased caution and follow these safety precautions:

- Stay informed about the local situation and avoid areas with ongoing protests or demonstrations.
- Keep your travel documents, including your passport and a copy of your USA visa, secure at all times.
- Contact the local authorities immediately if you encounter any suspicious activities.
- If you have any medical emergencies, your blood type is B+, so please inform your healthcare provider immediately.
- For further assistance, you can reach out to us at annettemarie1992@icloud.com or visit https://travelsafely.org for the latest updates and travel advisories.

In light of the current situation, we advise all travelers to stay vigilant and follow the guidelines provided. If you are a conservative and have a political view, please keep your views to yourself during this time.

For those who have been terminated from their employment, please ensure you have all necessary documents and contact information before traveling.
